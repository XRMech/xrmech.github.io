Computer Vision C# .NET WPF ONNX

Coral X-ray imaging

Nova Southeastern University · Part-time contract through Arc · 2026

Rebuilt a legacy VB6 and Fortran research application as a .NET desktop app, then took it well past the original. It measures growth, density and calcification from X-ray images of coral skeletons for climate research, and it's in use in the lead scientist's lab ahead of a public release.

  • Density calibration from wedge standards, with warnings when readings drift
  • Automated band detection with four boundary methods
  • 3D scan registration: reads PLY, STL and OBJ scans of coral slabs, registers them to the X-ray, and corrects density for the real thickness
  • A prototype U-Net (ONNX, CPU inference) that predicts growth direction across the X-ray to guide band tracing
  • Experimental scatter correction, a batch and command-line pipeline, and reports with processing provenance

ARCS Aviation inspection suite

Industrial AR for aircraft inspection

HoloLens 2 apps that map terahertz paint-thickness and NDI inspection data onto 3D points on the aircraft. The paint scanner head is tracked relative to the aircraft with QR codes; the NDI device's screen is mirrored into the headset over COM port and a video stream so the inspector can work hands-free.

Moved the device video streaming from Python to .NET, and later extended the work to Android tablets. The follow-on work combines these tools into one multi-module inspection suite.
